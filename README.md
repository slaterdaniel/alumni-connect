# Overview
Alumni Connect is an application to be adopted by schools, serving as a Connection Center for current students and alumni, enabling the exploration of career pathways, building colleagueship, and creating professional opportunities for members of the community.

The primary features of the app include a network of users comprised of students and alumni to foster relationships and mentorship opportunities by creating a hub for direct communication between members. 

Additionally, Alumni Connect will house a digital Alumni Newspaper that allows students and alumni to stay informed about upcoming events, publications, and other important updates within the community.
